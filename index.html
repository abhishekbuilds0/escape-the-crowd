CHAPTER ONE · FREE SAMPLE

The Illusion of Safety

Bheed ke saath chalna itna comfortable kyun lagta hai?

Socho ek din tum akele kisi crowded market mein khade ho. Sab log ek particular direction mein tez chal rahe hain. Tumhe pata bhi nahi ki wahan kya ho raha hai — koi accident, koi sale, ya sirf ek rumor. Lekin tumhare paanv khud-ba-khud unke saath chalne lagte hain. Kyun?

Kyunki bheed ek illusion deti hai — safety ka illusion. Jab hum bahut saare logon ko ek jaisa karte dekhte hain, hamara dimaag ek shortcut leta hai: "Itne log kar rahe hain, matlab sahi hi hoga." Ye koi weakness nahi hai — ye hazaron saal purani human wiring hai. Jab hamare ancestors jungle mein rehte the, group se alag hona matlab predator ka easy target banna. Akela hona khatarnak tha. Saath rehna zinda rehne ka formula tha.

Lekin aaj ka jungle different hai. Aaj koi sherr tumhe nahi khaayega agar tum apna alag rasta choose karo. Phir bhi wahi purana fear humare andar zinda hai — judgment ka fear, rejection ka fear, "log kya kahenge" ka fear. Isliye hum wahi college choose karte hain jo "sabse acha" mana jaata hai, wahi career jo family aur friends approve karte hain, wahi opinions rakhte hain jo humare group mein popular hain. Hum khud se ye sawaal kabhi poochte hi nahi — "Ye mujhe chahiye bhi hai, ya sirf isliye kar raha hoon kyunki baaki sab kar rahe hain?"

"Bheed ka sabse bada trap ye hai ki wo comfort deti hai, lekin growth nahi."

Jab tum bheed ke saath chalte ho, koi tumhe judge nahi karta — kyunki tum bhi wahi kar rahe ho jo sab kar rahe hain. Lekin jab tum apna alag rasta choose karte ho, judgment shuru ho jaata hai. Aur yahi judgment ka dar hai jo maximum logon ko unki poori zindagi bheed mein hi rakh deta hai.

Real safety bheed mein nahi hoti — real safety self-awareness mein hoti hai. Jab tumhe pata ho ki tum kya chahte ho aur kyun chahte ho, tab bheed ka opinion tumhe hila nahi sakta.
